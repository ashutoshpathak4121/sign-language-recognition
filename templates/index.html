    Tips for better results

    • Lighting: Ensure even light on your hands with minimal dark shadows.
    • Position: Hold your hand near the center of the frame against a plain background.
    • Clarity: Keep your hand still for a moment to prevent motion blur.

    About this tool and its limits

    • ASL Alphabet (A–Z): Trained on static handshapes. Letters J and Z use dynamic motion, so single-frame detection is best-effort.
    • Practice Partner: This is an educational tool and does not replace a certified human sign language interpreter.
    • Privacy: Your image is sent to our server to find the sign, used only for this result, and replaced by the next image.
